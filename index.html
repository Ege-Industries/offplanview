Questions from developers.

What happens on the 15-minute call?

You briefly tell me about your project, I show you an example and tell you honestly whether it's a fit. If it is, you get a free preview within 48 hours. No sales pressure.

What do you need from us?

Floor plans as PDF or CAD, elevations, your price list and renderings if you have them. For the free preview, floor plans and elevations are enough.

The project is already half sold. Is it still worth it?

Especially then. The last units are often the hardest to sell. The site only shows what is still available, and your team calls the enquiries that can actually buy first.

We already have a company website. Do we still need a project website?

Yes. The project website complements your company site and runs on its own address or on your domain, for example project.your-company.com.

How current is the availability?

As current as your sales team. An apartment is set to reserved or sold with one click and shows up immediately.

Does the 3D model work on phones?

Yes. The site is built for phones and adapts its effects to the device's performance.

What does a 3D project website cost?

Regular price €6,900 one-off plus €249 per month until sold out. The first three developers get the pilot price of €3,900. The preview is always free.

Do you work with developers outside Germany?

Yes. Everything happens online: plans by email, calls via Google Meet, the website runs anywhere in the world.
